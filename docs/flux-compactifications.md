# Flux compactifications

### reviews
[Lectures on Gauged Supergravity and Flux Compactifications](http://arxiv.org/abs/0808.4076)  
by Henning Samtleben (0808.4076 [hep-th], 48 pages)

[Physics of String Flux Compactifications](http://www.arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0701050)  
by Frederik Denef, Michael R. Douglas, Shamit Kachru (hep-th/0701050, 39 pages)  
a qualitative review of flux compactifications of string theory, focusing on broad physical implications and statistical methods of analysis

[Four-dimensional String Compactifications with D-Branes, Orientifolds and Fluxes](http://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0610327)  
by R. Blumenhagen, B. Körs, D. Lüst, S. Stieberger (hep-th/0610327, 331 pages)

[Flux Compactification](http://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0610102)  
by Michael R. Douglas, Shamit Kachru (hep-th/0610102, 69 pages, 3 figures)

[Flux compactifications in string theory: a comprehensive review](http://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0509003)  
by Mariana Graña (hep-th/0509003, 85 pages, 2 figures)

[TASI/PiTP/ISS Lectures on Moduli and Microphysics](http://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0405068)  
by Eva Silverstein (hep-th/0405068, 35 pages)
