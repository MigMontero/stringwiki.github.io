# Branes

see also [D branes](d-branes.md)

### reviews
[Supermembranes](http://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9611203)  
by M. J. Duff (hep-th/9611203, 76 pages)

[Branes, Calibrations and Supergravity](http://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0305074)  
by Jerome P. Gauntlett (hep-th/0305074, 47 pages)

[Intersecting brane solutions in string and M-theory](http://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0210157)  
by Douglas J. Smith (hep-th/0210157, 84 pages)

[Lectures on Branes in Curved Backgrounds](http://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0209241)  
by Volker Schomerus (hep-th/0209241, 109 pages, 11 figures)

[Brane Theory Solitons](http://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0004039)  
by P.K. Townsend (hep-th/0004039, 31 pages)

[String/M-branes for Relativists](http://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/9908045)  
by Donald Marolf  (gr-qc/9908045, 50 pages, 6 figures)  
This brief introduction to branes conveys some basic aspects of brane physics and
perspectives on string theory to those trained in general
relativity.

[Lectures on Supergravity p-branes](http://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9701088)  
by K.S. Stelle (hep-th/9701088, 53 pages, 4 figures)

[Black Holes and Solitons in String Theory](http://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9710046)  
by Donam Youm (hep-th/9710046, 240 pages)  
An encylclopedic review of black-hole and black-brane solutions.

[BPS Branes in Supergravity](http://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9803116)  
by K.S. Stelle (hep-th/9803116, 99 pages, 7 figures)  
Stelle provides a useful introduction to brane solutions in supergravity, containing many details of
supersymmetry, Kaluza-Klein reduction, and low-velocity scattering of branes.

[Gaugings and other supergravity tools of p-brane physics](http://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0102114)  
by Pietro Fre' (hep-th/0102114, 122 pages)  

[Branes and Brane Worlds in M-Theory](http://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0110084)  
by J.F. Vazquez-Poritz (hep-th/0110084, 189 pages, 15 figures)

[Supergravity, Brane Dynamics and String Duality](http://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9811101)  
by P. West (hep-th/9811101, 109 pages, 2 figures)

[Brane Physics in M Theory](http://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9807171)  
by Riccardo Argurio (hep-th/9807171, 206 pages, PhD thesis)

[Black Holes and Solitons in String Theory](http://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9210050)  
by Ashoke Sen (hep-th/9210050, 24 pages)

[Supersymmetric String Solitons](http://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9112030)  
by C.G.Callan Jr., J.A.Harvey, A.E.Strominger (hep-th/9112030, 42 pages)

[Instantons and Solitons in Heterotic String Theory](http://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9109052)  
by Curtis G. Callan, Jr. (hep-th/9109052, 24 pages)
