# Dualities

see also [Gauge Theory](gauge-theory.md), [Supersymmetry](supersymmetry.md)

### reviews
[Lectures on Superstring and M Theory Dualities](http://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9607201)  
by John H. Schwarz (hep-th/9607201, 67 pages, 1 figure)

[Target Space Duality in String Theory](http://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9401139)  
by A. Giveon, M. Porrati, E. Rabinovici (hep-th/9401139, 151 pages, 10 figures)

[U-duality and M-Theory](http://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9809039)  
by N.A. Obers, B. Pioline (hep-th/9809039, 132 pages, 37 tables)  
While primarily a review of U-duality, this work includes an important discussion of dualities in matrix theory.

[String Theory and Duality](http://arxiv.org/abs/math.AG/9809004)  
by Paul S. Aspinwall (math.AG/9809004, 10 pages)

[Les Houches Lectures on Fields, Strings and Duality](http://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9703136)  
by R. Dijkgraaf (hep-th/9703136, 152 pages, 31 figures)

[Lectures on Strings and Dualities](http://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9702201)  
by Cumrun Vafa (hep-th/9702201, 53 pages)

[Duality in String Theory](http://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9612192)  
by Stefan Forste, Jan Louis (hep-th/9612192, 38 pages, 2 figures)

[K3 Surfaces and String Duality](http://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9611137)  
by Paul S. Aspinwall (hep-th/9611137, 109 pages, 11 figures)

[An Introduction to T-Duality in String Theory](http://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9410237)  
by E. Alvarez, L. Alvarez-Gaume, Y. Lozano (hep-th/9410237, 1 figure)

[Magnetic Monopoles, Duality, and Supersymmetry](http://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9603086)

[Duality in Quantum Field Theory (and String Theory).](http://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9709180)

[Duality and Global Symmetries](http://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9706210)
