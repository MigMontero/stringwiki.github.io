# The dS/CFT correspondence

### reviews
[Les Houches Lectures on De Sitter Space](http://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0110007)  
by Marcus Spradlin, Andrew Strominger, Anastasia Volovich (hep-th/0110007, 29 pages, 8 figures)  
This work reviews speculations as to how gauge/gravity
dualities might extend to de Sitter space.  Useful background on de Sitter space is included.

[Classical Geometry of De Sitter Spacetime: An Introductory Review](http://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0212326)  
by Yoonbai Kim, Chae Young Oh, Namil Park (hep-th/0212326, 40 pages, 12 figures)
