# Matrix theory

### reviews
[Lectures on localization and matrix models in supersymmetric Chern-Simons-matter theories](http://arxiv.org/abs/1104.0783)  
by Marcos Marino (1104.0783 [hep-th], 71 pages, 7 figures)

[Algebraic methods in random matrices and enumerative geometry](http://arxiv.org/abs/0811.3531)  
by Bertrand Eynard (SPhT), Nicolas Orantin (CERN) (0811.3531 [hep-th], review article, Latex, 139 pages, many figures)

[Review of Matrix Theory](http://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9712072)  
by Daniela Bigatti, Leonard Susskind (hep-th/9712072, 45 pages, 2 figures)

[Matrix Models](http://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0506186)  
by C. Sochichiu (hep-th/0506186, 38 pages)

[M(atrix) Theory: Matrix Quantum Mechanics as a Fundamental Theory](http://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0101126)  
by Washington Taylor (hep-th/0101126, 56 pages, 3 figures)  
Taylor makes an effort to make his review accessible, to cover a broad range of topics, and to describe how matrix theory relates to other models.

[The M(atrix) model of M-theory](http://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0002016)  
by Washington Taylor (hep-th/0002016, 80 pages, 4 figures)

[Introduction to M(atrix) theory and noncommutative geometry](http://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0012145)  
by A. Konechny, A. Schwarz (hep-th/0012145)

[Introduction to M(atrix) theory and noncommutative geometry, Part II](http://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0107251)  
by A. Konechny, A. Schwarz (hep-th/0107251, 34 pages)

[TASI Lectures on Matrix Theory](http://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9911068)  
by Tom Banks (hep-th/9911068, 47 pages)  
This review by a founder of matrix theory emphasizes discrete light-cone quantization and
compactifications.
