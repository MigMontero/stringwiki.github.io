# The AdS/CFT correspondence

see also [D branes](d-branes.md), [Holography](holography.md)

### books
**D-Branes**  
by Clifford Johnson (CUP, 2003)

**Black Holes, Information and the String Theory Revolution**  
by Leonard Susskind and James Lindsay (World Scientific Publishing, 2005)

### reviews for beginners
[Supersymmetric Gauge Theories and the AdS/CFT Correspondence](http://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0201253)  
by Eric D'Hoker, Daniel Z. Freedman (hep-th/0201253, 145 pages, 14 figures)  
This review covers all the basics in detail, with a calculational focus.

[Large N Field Theories, String Theory and Gravity](http://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9905111)  
by O. Aharony, S.S. Gubser, J. Maldacena, H. Ooguri, Y. Oz (hep-th/9905111, 261 pages, 42 figures)  
This is the canonical review of AdS/CFT.  The gauge-theory perspective is emphasized, but many supergravity issues are also discussed.
