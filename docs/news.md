# News

### 1st December 2006: Change to arXiv identifiers
The [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/) is [changing its identifier policy](http://arxiv.org/new#ids_2006).  From 1st January 2007 all preprints
will have an identifier 0701.0001 instead of hep-th/0701001.  This is
being done to increase the available identifiers for each month to
9999.  The removal of the category from the identifier, such as
hep-th, will also make multiple classification easier.
